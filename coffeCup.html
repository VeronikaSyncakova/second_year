Star bean

About

Award winning coffee shop simulation. You are playing as a space barista and you want to keep the customers happy, earn money and develop your skills through different achievements.

Some things my team is proud of include radial menus with easing functions, pre-caching of assets for faster execution, finite state machine, singleton pattern.

This was voluntary project in a team of 5 for a competition Called Games Fleadh that is sponsored by companies such as EA. Our team consisted of 3 programmers, one artist and one sound designer. Everything in the game was made by us.

The game won 2 awards - The Best User Interface and The Best Sound Design

Technology

C++, SFML library, text file
